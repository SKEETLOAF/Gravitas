# Equipment and cosmetics
Updated 2026-11-26. Overall itemization direction approved; exact rolls and costs open.

Prestige labels denote intended progression ranges/release windows, not mandatory lineage prestige; shared system access uses Character Level and usually adventure-stage clears. See PROGRESSION.md.

## Item identity
Equipment does not require individual wearable models; cosmetics determine appearance. Shared weapon-family visuals recommended. Class-relevant loot picked up during adventure. Gear sets grow stronger and more varied with progression. Item level and rarity separate. Existing slots in [Combat](COMBAT-FOUNDATIONS.md). Primary equipped weapon contributes 100% stats; secondary contributes 50% independent of wielded weapon. Starting multiplier subject to balance testing. Future items/relics (relics introduced in Prestige II) may grant bonus effective talent ranks beyond normal investment caps; linear node scaling supports this, no specific affixes approved.
- Standard equipment has slot-specific primary attribute; accessories may roll any primary attribute.
- Secondary attributes may be any stat at smaller relative scale, with slot-favored probabilities permitted, never mandatory restrictions. Primary/secondary overlap and repeated secondary types allowed. Each rarity adds exactly one secondary line.

## Equipment development
Slot enhancement carries investment across replacements. Item-specific attributes remain on individual item. Mixed set bonuses supported; proposed 2/4/6 thresholds where appropriate, early stat benefits and later mechanical synergy. No named sets/values finalized.
Recommended ordinary drops, dungeon set rewards, targeted acquisition/crafting through dungeon materials, salvage, class-aware loot preference across equipped classes. All equipped gear is shared and unchanged when switching classes. No offline equipment. Default auto-pickup and recommended equip; optional filters and opt-in automatic salvage, protect locked/saved items. No mandatory extra gem/socket systems established.
Reforge/Recast (Prestige 0 unlock, owner-confirmed): use a shared reforge material to change all or selected attribute identities, reroll existing value to seek higher roll. Show current value and min/max ranges plus replacement pool before rolling; preview costs and probabilities, consider line locks and retain-old option. Exact rules and whether Reforge and Recast are separate operations remain open.

Equipment Enhancements are slated for Prestige II (owner direction, responding to the proposed conditional equipment enchantment system). Exact mechanics and naming remain open; distinguish this future system from ordinary slot enhancement and Prestige 0 Reforge/Recast, neither of which is moved by this decision.

## Cosmetics
Titles and cosmetics use the same seven rarity tiers as equipment (owner-confirmed). Rarity increases their statistical bonuses. Owner revises the working bonus ladder to +0.5 percentage points per ordinary tier and +0.75 percentage points at Mythic and Secret: Common 0.5%, Uncommon 1%, Rare 1.5%, Epic 2%, Legendary 2.5%, Mythic 3.25%, Secret 4%. These are starting balance targets. Recommend designated-attribute bonuses rather than all attributes simultaneously. Exact base, increment, target attributes, per-item versus collection/equipped bonus behavior, aggregate caps and stacking remain open. Avoid interpreting the ladder as an uncapped permanent multiplier for every owned item.
Direct purchases and summons, earnable free spins, small statistical benefit. Recommended capped ownership/wardrobe bonus independent of currently worn outfit, attainable via free cosmetics as well. Avoid uncapped paid stacking and forcing specific appearance. Categories may include outfits, hair/accessories, weapon skins, auras, animations and companion skins. Bonus type/cap, acquisition odds, duplicates, slots and cosmetic catalog open.

## Titles
Owner confirms earned titles grant small statistical bonuses comparable in scale/purpose to cosmetic bonuses. Both categories use the shared equipment rarity ladder and proposed bonus progression above. Recommend ownership-based bonuses independent of the displayed title, with cumulative limits to prevent uncontrolled stacking; exact ownership/equip behavior, caps, acquisition categories and values remain open. Class Mastery, achievements and milestones are established title reward sources (PROGRESSION.md).
