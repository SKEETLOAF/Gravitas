# Economy and currencies
Updated 2026-11-26 | Proposed architecture, pending owner review.

## Established guardrails
Paid acceleration is permitted; free players must have realistically attainable equivalent power and guaranteed acquisition paths. No required payment for ordinary progression. Free cosmetic summons, Stardust from unwanted summon disassembly, and optional boss-respawn skips are established. See ITEMIZATION.md, SKILLS-AND-COLLECTION.md and WORLD-AND-IDLE.md for their mechanics.

## Proposed wallet
| Currency | Acquisition | Sinks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gold | Combat, quests, eligible offline rewards, selling/salvaging where appropriate | Slot enhancements, skill and companion upgrades, crafting, pet ascension |
| Premium currency (name TBD) | Earned milestones, events, recurring rewards, direct Robux purchase if adopted | Summons, cosmetics, convenience, guaranteed selection offers |
| Stardust | Disassemble unwanted eligible summon results at any rank; max-rank overflow | Selected summons, duplicate-equivalent materials, selectors, other designated exchange stock |
No separate generic currency for every progression system. Premium currency being earnable and purchasable is proposed, not confirmed.

## Tickets and materials
Separate earnable Skill, Pet, Mount and Cosmetic summon tickets; premium currency as an optional substitute after explicit confirmation. Ticket pools, generation eligibility and conversion rules open. Materials grouped by purpose: equipment enhancements/crafting, skill levels, companion upgrades, pet ascension, dungeon-targeted acquisition and event rewards. Prefer shared material families with tiered grades where possible rather than a distinct currency per class, map or pet. Reforge and relic resources deferred with those systems.

## Acquisition and expenditure
- Online adventure: reliable gold, XP, ordinary equipment and relevant materials; class-aware pickup. Offline: reduced XP/gold/eligible materials, capped 24 hours, no equipment.
- Dungeon mobs: ongoing farming; boss encounter every ten minutes, targeted set loot and possible crafting materials. Boss timer skip for Robux confirmed; earned skip tokens recommended.
- First clears, promotion milestones and quests: substantial one-time rewards and relevant summon tickets. Recurring activities and events: reliable ticket/material income and earnable premium currency. Exact cadence and quantities open.
- Gear enhancement remains attached to slots; gear salvage and targeted dungeon acquisition reduce dependence on luck. Pet ascension uses an eligible donor plus resources; skill/companion star ranks use duplicates and potential equivalent exchange materials.
- Stardust shop: predictable recurring useful stock and visible costs; no requirement to disassemble a favorite or max it before exchanging unwanted copies. Direct guaranteed selectors complement pity, not replace ordinary free income.

## Proposed economy policies
- Keep normal upgrades affordable through ordinary play; reserve long-term sinks for optional optimization, collection and higher progression tiers. Avoid making every upgrade require premium currency.
- Use transparent, non-expiring compatible-banner pity and target guarantees. Publish odds and applicable changes; paid random items require Roblox policy eligibility checks and disclosures. Validate current platform rules before implementation.
- Avoid uncapped paid accumulation of cosmetic combat bonuses; free cosmetics can reach the same capped benefit. No automatic premium spending.
- No trading system assumed at launch. Direct player trading, gifting, marketplace, item binding and paid-item restrictions are open; recommend deciding after reward economy and multiplayer design to prevent exploit and inflation problems.
- Exact exchange rates, gold curves, material tiers, summon odds, pity counts, price points, purchase limits, free-player milestones and inflation simulations are later balance work. No numeric economy model or gameplay code implemented.
