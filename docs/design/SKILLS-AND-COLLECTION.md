# Skills, Energy, collection and monetization
Updated 2026-11-26. Owner decisions and recommendations separated. No implementation or numerical balance approved.

## Established owner direction
- Energy is the universal skill resource. All skills including ultimates use Energy. No universal ultimate gauge; particular skills/ultimates may have stacks, special activation conditions or gauges.
- Hack-and-slash real-time attacks proceed as fast as weapon fire/attack rate and skill cooldowns allow. Detailed combat flow deferred by owner. On incoming hits, automatic defensive actions (block/evasion) are probabilistic based on stats versus applicable counter stats.
- Functional base skills are acquired at class base/unlock; alternate skills and skill upgrades can be summoned. Skills use cooldown after full use or charges that replenish over time/after events. Skill development via upgrade materials and summoned duplicates; star-rank advancement via summons. Unwanted summon results can be voluntarily disassembled for Stardust without reaching maximum rank; maxed duplicates still convert to Stardust exchange currency for selected rewards (Limbus Thread-like upgrade/exchange materials as inspiration).
- Selectable skill summon focus for better odds at desired skills, pity with different levels.
- Pets and mounts are available from the start. Each has one usable skill; pets also perform their own basic attacks as visible mini partners. Mounts primarily enhance the character, not a second independent fighter. Both have distinct appearances, rarities, ranges of aid, summons and duplicate upgrades. Summon offerings improve as regions or class promotions are reached; exact gate and pool rules open. This supersedes delayed pet/mount unlocks. Go Go Muffin inspiration.
- Relics are intended powerful unique upgrades that may complete/synergize builds, but owner explicitly defers detailed design to a future update.
- Monetization intent: spenders can buy convenient acceleration, but equivalent power should not be nearly impossible or excessively drawn out for free players. Paid access policy, rates, costs and caps not decided.

## Recommended mechanics (not established)
- Shared Energy across class swaps, no basic attack Energy cost, passive regeneration in combat plus modest normalized on-hit recovery, faster out-of-combat recovery if needed. Avoid attack speed double scaling by time-normalizing on-hit recovery; multi-hit attacks count per action or capped time window. No fixed costs, capacity or regeneration figures.
- Defensive resolution: evasion first, block second, then mitigation. Successful defense visibly animates where safe, without interrupting all outgoing actions. Candidate counter stats Accuracy, Block Penetration, Armor/Resistance Penetration, Effect Potency. Define caps and counters, boss exceptions, physical/magical behavior, manual action interactions later.
- Aggro is an attribute (owner-confirmed), exact threat formula open.
- Ordinary skill levels from earned materials separate from duplicate star ranks. Base kits usable without RNG. Alternate skills offer sidegrades; duplicate ranks can improve potency or selected utility, not invalidate base skill. Existing skills and classes remain available after promotion consistent with earlier class expansion. Skill ranks, copy thresholds and rarity tiers TBD.
- Summon pools: standard, class-selective, wishlist/featured, separate pet/mount pools. Soft pity, hard rarity pity, target guarantee, carryover between compatible banners. Distinguish rarity guarantees and selected item guarantees. Earnable selector/duplicate-equivalent materials and Stardust exchanges provide deterministic fallback; no fixed pull counts, rates, currency yield or shop rotation decided.
- Stardust from voluntary disassembly of unwanted summon results, including before max rank, as well as max-rank overflow. Recommend allowing spare-copy disassembly without deleting learned skills, lock and confirmation protections, and clear exchange values. Exact exchange rates, cross-pool eligibility and treatment of unique owned items remain open.
- Pet: recommend one visible active companion with basic attacks and its one signature skill; mount: recommend one equipped mount with modest player enhancement, travel appearance and its one usable skill (player-centric rather than a second independent fighter). Skill timing, resource costs and activation rules open. Number of slots, mounted combat, collection bonuses, rarity distribution, ownership, collection limits and acquisition rates open. Avoid forcing every pet/mount to be maxed to compete.
- Free acquisition: repeatable earned pulls and deterministic exchange/selector sources, achievable upgrade timeline, identical gameplay caps for paid/free, optional direct guaranteed purchases for eligible items, avoid power exclusively locked behind paid random items. Pricing and free progression targets require economy design and simulations.
- Roblox paid-random-items compliance: if Robux or Robux-purchasable currency accesses random rewards, disclose all outcomes and numerical odds before purchase, dynamically reflect pity/rate-up odds, enforce PolicyService eligibility server-side and applicable trading restrictions. Provide eligible non-random acquisition alternatives and keep genuinely earned-only systems compliant. Check current rules before implementation. ## Progression-gated summon offerings (proposal)
Start with an accessible beginner pool and starter pet/mount options. New regions and/or class promotion milestones unlock additional eligible summons and more diverse effects rather than locking the whole system. Consider region-tiered selectable pools with earlier pools retained, compatible pity carryover, and no forced replacement of earlier companions; distinguish rarity from raw power and avoid automatically devaluing early upgrades. Whether region, promotion or both governs pool expansion remains undecided. No odds, stat values, starter acquisition method or summon tier counts confirmed.
