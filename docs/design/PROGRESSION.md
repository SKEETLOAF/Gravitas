# Character and class progression
Updated 2026-11-26. Overall model approved.

## Three layers
1. Character Level: permanent account-wide level for the single persistent character (owner-confirmed). Used for claimable character-level rewards, early and ongoing progression/system unlocks, equipment eligibility, class-specific inherent attribute scaling, level-gap penalties and percentage-based stat scaling. No prestige reset. Character Level is intended to progress at a broadly comparable pace to the player's leading lineage's continuous progression, not to match the displayed stage level or advance one-for-one. Global XP from combat, quests, stages, dungeons and eligible idle rewards remains the working source model; Character XP is granted once at 100% regardless of equipped classes (owner-approved). Exact source eligibility, curves, rewards, level-gap and percentage-scaling formulas open. Lineage stage levels independently gate class evolution and award lineage talent points; see below. Ordinary class swapping does not reset progression. Owner establishes inherent class-specific base attribute scaling with Character Level, distinct from Class Mastery, gear, talents and Class Traits. Foundations use an 18-point main-attribute Scaling Allotment, with one additional allotment point per prestige (owner-confirmed: P0 18, P I 19, P II 20, future sequential P III 21). This is a class-profile budget, not a player talent-point grant. Initial hybrids have 21-point P III-equivalent scaling at acquisition (owner-confirmed); special-class budgets remain open. Allotment is distributed across Health, Energy, ATK, MATK, DEF and Magical Defense. Growth weights display as F=1, D=2, C=3, B=4, A=5, S=6. Ordinary focus caps at A; S is reserved for exceptional focus at an additional trade-off. Approved S-grade trade-off: each S costs 7 budget points for weight 6 (one-point surcharge, so raw weights sum to allotment minus one for one S). Growth weights are not fixed stat values. Soft attributes grow modestly through Character Level; proficient attributes have roughly comparable natural growth across classes, while non-proficient attributes grow much more slowly. Approved approximate soft-attribute growth reference weights: 20 proficient or 5 non-proficient natural growth units against 75 external progression units at a comparable benchmark. The two natural rates are alternatives, not additive. Weights are not literal realized percentages or hard caps. Equipment and talents lead external growth, pets/mounts supplement, Style Score milestone bonuses are modest, and relics may be build-defining later. Actual normalization and curves remain for numerical balancing. Exact natural growth rates remain open; foundational proficiency profiles in CLASSES.md. Use data-driven profiles; equal weights alone do not guarantee equal combat value. Ordinary class swapping never resets progression; Character Level governs natural class attribute scaling; lineage stage levels govern class evolution and lineage talent points. Exact source eligibility, XP curves and per-class allotment distribution remain open. Owner approves primary 100% plus distinct secondary 50% inherent class growth, or 150% of a single profile when specializing. Do not reduce growth contributions for overlapping grades or affinities. Talent effect contributions use the same multipliers; binary-node interpretation under review; weapon attributes remain 100% primary and 50% secondary. Apply to class-derived growth, independent of active class; do not duplicate shared base stats or multiply shared external bonuses. Exact curves and deeper stat allocation remain open.
2. Class Mastery: independent progression for every learned discipline, retained permanently. Rewards: titles with small statistical bonuses (owner-confirmed; see ITEMIZATION.md), achievements/milestones and their rewards, evolution eligibility, class-specific cosmetics (owner-confirmed), and existing inherent Class Trait unlock/upgrade milestones. Ordinary class skills are acquired through the skill summoning system and functional starter kit, not Mastery rewards. Talent points remain exclusively lineage-level rewards. Mastery and Lineage XP are distinct progression tracks earned through overlapping activities, not the same XP balance. Each class has one passive trait leveled through Class Mastery requirements and shared Ascension items; thresholds, costs and level caps open. Owner confirms Class Mastery XP is split evenly (50% each) between two distinct equipped classes regardless of active time or combat participation. With one distinct equipped class, including specialization, recommend granting its full 100% share without a bonus multiplier. New class gets functional base kit and retains general character progression. Catch-up approved: underdeveloped classes/lineages advance faster toward the player's developed progression, including their evolution levels; benchmark, source eligibility and taper formula open. Catch-up accelerates earning levels, not automatic prestige unlocks.
3. Promotions: mastery threshold, relevant adventure milestone (already attained counts), promotion trial achievable via ordinary auto-combat if the player's build is sufficiently strong (owner-confirmed). Active repositioning and encounter inputs can offer an advantage; exact trial mechanics and power threshold open. Unlock distinct independently usable discipline, retain earlier classes. Catch-up accelerates mastery but does not skip requirements. Initial detailed roster scope two promotions per foundation (15 ordinary classes including foundations); owner confirms ordinary P III evolutions for all five lineages in the P III content window alongside hybrids, with names and kits deferred (20 ordinary classes through P III). P III stage cap working 120 and Scaling Allotment 21; Each lineage independently reaches its stage cap to qualify for the next Class Change, which resets only that lineage's stage level to 1 and raises its cap. Core lineage evolutions are directly sequential (owner-confirmed), preserving earlier classes and transitive compatible ordinary skill inheritance. Combat identities must be diverse within the shared lineage rather than direct upgrades. Working ordinary evolution eligibility accepted: reach lineage cap, meet Class Mastery requirement, clear required adventure stage (previous clears count), complete evolution trial. Exact thresholds, names and later caps remain open. Gunslinger moves into the sequential Ranger lineage with firearm access; as P II after Hunter (accepted working rule). Hybrids are unique classes with independent progression and talent budgets analogous to special classes (owner direction); parent lineages determine pairing and talent affinity, not the hybrid's independent talent budget. Owner adds 50/50 parent-lineage progression alongside the hybrid's own XP. Hybrid's independent progression retains the normal class-slot XP rules; each parent receives 50% progression XP regardless of the hybrid's equipped slot (owner-confirmed). Neither parent is primary or secondary. These are track-specific rates, not subdivisions of one XP pool. Independent mastery and one TP per eligible level gained remain. Parent overlap/deduplication, exact eligibility and source rules remain open. Owner confirms a one-time starting hybrid talent allotment equivalent to entering its corresponding prestige (P III initially), then normal TP earning. Proposed first two sections of both parent trees prefilled for 120 TP from working P III entry budget 223 TP, leaving 103; allocation and cross-tree rules open. Owner confirms initial hybrid level cap 120 (not cumulative 240) and P III-equivalent 21-point Scaling Allotment from acquisition. Working starting level 1; hybrid acquisition requires reaching P II in both parent lineages and completing a hybrid trial. XP follows P III progression rates, with no hybrid catch-up during P III; catch-up may become available around P IV or later (owner direction), exact benchmark, rates, trial conditions and numerical curves open. No mandatory earlier prestige stages. Special families (Sword Saint, Summoner) use shorter, simpler variant-based advancement instead of long mandatory promotion ladders, retaining competitive scaling through universal progression. Exact family mastery, variant unlock requirements, talent access and kit ownership open; see CLASSES.md.

## Class Change / Prestige (owner direction)
Class evolution uses Class Change/Prestige stages, each beginning at displayed level 1 with a higher maximum level. Working caps: foundation 60, Class Change I 80, Class Change II 100, then approximately +20 per future stage. Exact caps/XP curves are tuning decisions. This supersedes the earlier no-prestige recommendation. Ordinary combat/loadout swapping is not a Class Change and never resets a level. Earlier disciplines remain accessible.
Owner confirms independent lineage advancement: the stage level tracks eligibility for the next prestige in that respective class lineage. Prestiging one lineage does not advance or reset any other lineage. Underdeveloped lineages benefit substantially from catch-up, but must earn their own levels and qualify for their own Class Changes. All accumulated inherent attribute growth, earned talent points, equipment and eligibility, skills, mastery, pet-generation access, adventure progress and previously unlocked content are retained; only that lineage's displayed stage level resets. Track cumulative progression separately from the displayed stage level. Owner directs a continuing XP progression curve rather than resetting XP requirements at prestige: later level-ups should demand appropriately higher XP than early ones. Working interpretation of the owner's 60-to-61 example: with current 60/80/100 stage caps, P0 level 40 to 41 corresponds to reference 40 to 41, P I level 1 to 2 to reference 60 to 61, P I level 79 to 80 to reference 138 to 139, and P II level 1 to 2 to reference 139 to 140. Reference level = displayed lineage level + sum of (completed stage cap minus 1) across earlier stages. The reference level indexes a continuous XP curve, not the actual Character Level or an extra level-up award at Class Change. The cap boundary convention and exact XP curves/rates remain tuning decisions. Catch-up may accelerate lagging lineages without altering the account-level XP curve or resetting earned power. Existing promotion trial/adventure/mastery requirements remain working proposals to reconcile against level caps. Character Level is an independent permanent account-wide level; Class Mastery remains individual-class development. Owner-approved distribution for eligible XP rewards: Character XP 100% once account-wide; Lineage XP primary 100%, secondary 50%; Class Mastery XP split evenly between distinct equipped classes. Each track uses its own base XP reward/curve; these percentages do not divide a single shared XP pool. Catch-up may independently accelerate lagging lineage levels and individual mastery. When both slots refer to the same lineage, grant 100% Lineage XP once rather than duplicate awards (accepted working rule); specialization likewise grants a single full progression XP award. Hybrid XP goes to its own independent track, with additional 50/50 parent progression (50% each regardless of equipped slot; no secondary reduction to parent grants). When hybrid-parent grants overlap with direct or other hybrid contributions, exact deduplication remains open; recommend preserving the existing 100% ceiling per lineage. Hybrid's own catch-up does not apply during P III, with later eligibility around P IV or later. Recommend overlapping activities advance applicable tracks; exact quest/offline attribution and source rules open. Talent points are earned and owned independently by each lineage (owner-confirmed); exact source eligibility and per-class allotment distribution remain open.

## System unlock pacing
Owner confirms that shared progression systems unlock through permanent Character Level, with an adventure-stage-clear requirement for most systems. Prestige labels below describe intended pacing ranges and content-release windows, not mandatory lineage-prestige prerequisites for shared systems. Most foundational systems become available around Prestige 0; mounts are paced for very early Prestige I, replacing the from-start mount plan. Pets retain their existing from-start direction. Exact Character Level and stage thresholds remain open. Unlocks are character-wide and persist; do not require each lineage to unlock the same shared system again. Class evolution and class-specific acquisition retain their own eligibility rules; guild features use guild progression/management. Content must also have been released. Current owner-directed unlock map (exact level/stage thresholds open): Prestige 0 includes Reforge/Recast and pet moves; very early Prestige I introduces mounts; Prestige II introduces relics, equipment Enhancements (exact terminology/mechanics open; distinguish from existing slot enhancement), and Mythic Moves for evolved pets, with Pet Ascension a tentative Prestige II unlock; Prestige III introduces ordinary third evolutions alongside core hybrid classes, awakened skills and mount equipment (owner direction). A Stellar Path-like progression concept remains deferred to distant Prestige III+; Authority is the owner-selected system name; IV+ timing and mechanics are exploratory, not approved. Companion expeditions are accepted as a system; unlock timing and mechanics open. Tentative owner-directed rollout: launch Prestige 0 and I; target biweekly updates. Prestige II after two elapsed weeks (working timing), intermediate update after four weeks, Prestige III after six full elapsed weeks (start of week 7), with ordinary P III evolutions (names intentionally deferred) and five of the ten core hybrid classes in that update and the remaining five in a subsequent update (working target: eight elapsed weeks). Five hybrids per update is owner-confirmed; exact batch composition and intermediate-update content open. This is a planning target, not a promised launch date or fixed content delivery commitment. Validate content readiness, progression pacing and balance; prebuild and test major systems before launch. Guild feature unlocks depend on guild levels and management, not Character Level or individual prestige. Other exact stage gates and system choices remain open.

## Supporting progression
Skill-slot levels (Skill XP upgrades slots, retained on skill swaps), individual skill stars and max-star tier advancement separate from mastery; gear, pet and mount tracks distinct. Pets available from start; mounts unlock through Character Level and intended stage requirements, paced for very early Prestige I. Pet generation access through Character Level and appropriate stage requirements (exact gates open); mounts universal. Up to two class selections and two shared weapons, with slot unlock milestones open. Saved talent loadouts supported. Distinct individual class trees with two independent reconverging forks, linear universal trees with class-independent skill modifiers, entry bonuses and single-purchase nodes; level 15 unlock, 30 TP per completed section, P0 costs 1–3 and P I 2–6. See TALENTS.md and TALENT-CATALOG.md. One TP per eligible lineage level after unlock, retained on prestige; no point for reset. Working P0 cap 60 grants 45 TP, P I cap 80 adds 79, P II cap 100 adds 99, P III cap 120 adds 119. Three complete P0 trees would require 90 TP; additional grants not approved. Independent lineage budgets and hybrid tier-equivalent grant retained. No new currency or separate talent XP. See [TALENTS.md](TALENTS.md) for revised architecture and unresolved implementation. Selecting the same class twice provides specialization scaling and a second loadout (one Core and three ordinary actives per class), both existing selectable class passives, with ultimates deferred; the proposed specialization cooldown reduction bonus is removed. See COMBAT-SWITCHING.md. Class Change stage resets follow the rules above. See [Skills](SKILLS-AND-COLLECTION.md), [Itemization](ITEMIZATION.md), [Classes](CLASSES.md), and [proposed starter talent trees and hybrid point budget](TALENTS.md).

## Accessibility
Simple default view of current character/class, next promotion, recommended upgrade and adventure goal. Optional advanced stats and build analysis. Explainable reversible recommendations, no automatic premium spend or destruction of locked items. Avoid redundant XP tracks. Detailed UX later.

## Open
