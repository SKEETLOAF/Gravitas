# Combat flow, switching and shared loadout
Updated 2026-11-26. Owner-confirmed loadout architecture; detailed execution and balance open.

## Established
- Maximum two equipped class selections and two shared weapon slots for now. This is not a limit on learned classes; hybrid classes remain planned.
- Player selects two classes, two compatible weapons and each class's skills. Equipment, weapons, armor, accessories, pet, mount and future relics are shared and remain equipped when switching classes. Class selections have their own skill slots and selected talent loadouts. Five shared lineage trees rather than per-class trees are approved (CLASSES.md). Primary and secondary class slots contribute 100% and 50% talent ranks respectively (minimum one effective rank per invested secondary node), independent of active class; same-class specialization contributes 125% from one primary allocation. Inherent class-derived main and soft attribute growth follows the same 100%/50% or 125% specialization model, independent of active class; shared external stats are not multiplied. See CLASSES.md for growth and rank rules. Primary and secondary weapon slots similarly contribute 100% and 50% attributes independently of wielded weapon. Both selected class passives are available subject to associated class and trigger eligibility; only active class Trait functions, with explicitly persistent effects allowed. No resource refill on swap. Starting multipliers approved, subject to balance testing; overlapping-node resolution open.
- Selecting the same class twice is permitted and provides a scaling specialization buff instead of another kit or cooldown bank. Cooldown reduction is a candidate additional benefit; magnitude, affected coefficients and stacking are open. After class swapping unlocks, same-class specialization equips both of that class's two selectable passives; no duplicate active/ultimate kit or trait.
- Class swapping has a 0.5-second cooldown. Each weapon has one dedicated skill requiring its respective weapon to be wielded. Active weapon selection and whether weapon switching shares that cooldown are open.
- Swapping accesses the incoming class's selected skills. Recommend individual ability cooldowns continue during inactivity and never reset simply due to swaps; shared weapon skills retain their cooldowns across class swaps. Shared Health/Energy and persistent applied statuses/effects recommended, subject to explicit skill rules.

## Automatic combat and combo extension (owner-confirmed)
Combat should interweave basic strings, skills and class swaps into fluid extended sequences, with Dragon Ball: Sparking! ZERO pursuit/combos and Dragon Ball FighterZ Sparking-style extensions as feel references, not copied mechanics. It should not devolve into repeated skill-only casts or basic string -> swap -> basic string as the only optimal pattern.
- Auto switching uses available skills in the active class then switches to the other class's skills, or finishes a full basic-attack combo and switches to extend it. No elaborate tactical AI required for baseline behavior.
- A completed basic-attack chain has a brief recovery period during which another ordinary basic chain cannot immediately begin. A finisher may knock the target away; exact chain length, recovery duration and displacement vary by kit and remain open.
- Class switching strings into the completed combo and rushes the knocked-back target to continue the incoming class's combo rather than waiting through ordinary basic-chain recovery. The 0.5-second class swap cooldown still applies. Recommend a class-compatible pursuit transition (ranged styles may close to effective range), preserve valid target, and avoid rushing dead/invalid targets. For knockback-resistant bosses, a non-displacing continuation is recommended, not finalized.
- Eligible skills, including supportive skills, emit **Impulse**, a brief hit-stun pulse on activation against enemies within their individual effect area/range. Impulse creates combo continuity and animation-cancel opportunities; it need not deal HP damage. Intact enemy Armor prevents Impulse stagger/knockback even though the hit occurs. Ordinary break/CC rules apply.
- A skill used during a basic string can reset/extend the string, cancel eligible recovery and lead into another basic string or skill. If used following a completed basic string, Impulse knocks back eligible enemies; pursue to continue. This is a combo-state reset, not a skill cooldown reset.
- Skill-to-skill and skill-to-basic transitions may rush/chase displaced targets, close to a skill's effective range, or use appropriate ranged projectile homing. Preserve class/weapon identity and avoid teleporting or rushing invalid targets. Exact movement, tracking and range rules are open.
- Baseline auto-combat intermixes basic strings, eligible skill extensions and swaps through easy-to-author presets and player-created combat macros. Exact sequencing, readiness/Energy gates, cancel windows, pursuit distances, recovery rules and anti-lockdown tuning open; no fixed forced alternation or combo limit established. No automatic cooldown reset or free invulnerability implied.

## Automation and macros
Owner requires simple automation, especially easy developer preset authoring, with built-in presets and player-created macros. Recommended implementation: one data-driven, ordered action sequence format for both preset and custom macros, using ordinary combat actions (basic attack or full string, chosen skill, weapon skill, ultimate, class swap). A shared executor handles target validity, approach/pursuit, weapon compatibility, action recovery, Energy and cooldown readiness. Skip unavailable optional steps or fall back to basic attacks, rather than stall indefinitely; exact skip/wait behavior open. No external scripting or complex tactical AI required. Recommend slot-based references so presets remain reusable across classes, with optional class-specific sequences; advanced conditional commands, sharing, editor limits and unlock rules remain open. Macros use the same authoritative combat rules as normal automatic/manual inputs.

## Compatibility and execution
See [Weapons](WEAPONS.md) for class-linked weapon families. The two equipped weapons remain the same throughout class swaps; no per-class gear replacement. Recommend validating that each distinct selected class can use at least one equipped weapon. Whether swaps automatically select a compatible weapon, or require separate weapon selection, is open. Do not require both classes to share a weapon. Each weapon supplies one skill, not an additional class kit.

## Open
- Impulse eligibility, hit registration vs actual damage, range/timing, combo-state resets, Armor immunity and CC recovery interaction, ranged tracking and multi-target behavior. Prototype mixed strings against both ordinary and armored enemies.
- Manual swap triggers, macro editor and exact executor behavior, independent weapon swap rules, cooldown scope for weapon switching, cancel/recovery windows, target validity and animations.
- Passive trigger eligibility, talent preset switching restrictions, persistent effects, primary talent empowerment and other specialization scaling/potential cooldown reduction.
- Secondary weapon attribute multiplier, weapon skill activation/slot presentation, duplicate weapon eligibility.
- Slot unlock milestones and out-of-combat loadout editing restrictions.
- Prototype compare same-class specialization vs two distinct classes for uptime, survivability, role utility, automation and readability. Maintain server-authoritative cooldowns and prevent re-equipping/reset abuse.
