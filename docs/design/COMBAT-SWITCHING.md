# Combat flow, switching and shared loadout
Updated 2026-11-26. Owner-confirmed loadout architecture; detailed execution and balance open.

## Established
- Maximum two equipped class selections and two shared weapon slots for now. This is not a limit on learned classes; hybrid classes remain planned.
- Player selects two classes, two compatible weapons and each class's skills. Equipment, weapons, armor, accessories, pet, mount and future relics are shared and remain equipped when switching classes. Class selections have their own skill slots (one Core Skill and three ordinary actives per class, proposed five passive-skill slots per class, ultimates deferred) and selected talent loadouts. Distinct individual class trees with two independent reconverging forks, linear universal trees with class-independent skill modifiers, entry bonuses and single-purchase nodes; level 20 talent unlock (first TP at 21), 30 TP per P0/P I section and 50 TP per P II+ section; uniform templates by tree family, see TALENTS.md. See TALENTS.md and TALENT-CATALOG.md. Hybrids immediately access both parent trees, with independent tier-equivalent TP grant and normal subsequent earning. Proposed first two sections of both parent trees prefilled for 120 TP from working P III entry budget 331 TP (including equivalent entry point), leaving 211; allocation and cross-tree rules open. Earlier cross-tree gates/prices require reconciliation. Primary and secondary class slots contribute 100% and 50% talent effect strength respectively; specialization 150%. Numeric and qualitative binary-node interpretation remains open. Class-specific effects respect eligibility. Inherent class-derived main and soft growth uses 100% primary plus 50% distinct secondary regardless of shared affinities, or 150% for same-class specialization, independent of active class; shared external stats are not multiplied. See CLASSES.md for growth and rank rules. Primary and secondary weapon slots similarly contribute 100% and 50% attributes independently of wielded weapon. Both selected class passives are available subject to associated class and trigger eligibility; only active class Trait functions, with explicitly persistent effects allowed. No resource refill on swap. Starting multipliers approved, subject to balance testing. Overlapping same-lineage effects retain prior stacking intent, but binary-node and coefficient rules must be resolved in TALENTS.md.
- Same-class specialization retains 150% inherent growth/talents, both existing selectable class passives and two independent loadouts, each one Core and three ordinary active skills. One Trait, no specialization cooldown reduction. Five passive-skill slots per loadout proposed; duplicate stacking and trigger eligibility open. No ultimate slot for now. Skill levels belong to slots, not skills; equipped skills inherit slot level, retain their own stars and tiers. Exact slot investment sharing across class/loadout presets open. No Energy replenishment or cooldown reset; identical learned skill IDs recommended to share cooldown/charge state. Exact timing and duplicate placement open.
- Class swapping has a 0.5-second cooldown; removal of proposed specialization cooldown reduction is interpreted as removing the bonus, not removing this ordinary swap cooldown (specialization loadout swap timing to confirm). Each weapon has one dedicated skill, automatically drawing its respective equipped weapon on activation. Weapon changes alone do not reset recovery, combos or cooldowns. Exact transition windows open.
- Swapping accesses the incoming class's selected skills. Recommend individual ability cooldowns continue during inactivity and never reset simply due to swaps; shared weapon skills retain their cooldowns across class swaps. Shared Health/Energy and persistent applied statuses/effects recommended, subject to explicit skill rules.

## Automatic combat and combo extension (owner-confirmed)
Combat should interweave basic strings, skills and class swaps into fluid extended sequences, with Dragon Ball: Sparking! ZERO pursuit/combos and Dragon Ball FighterZ Sparking-style extensions as feel references, not copied mechanics. It should not devolve into repeated skill-only casts or basic string -> swap -> basic string as the only optimal pattern.
- Auto switching uses available skills in the active class then switches to the other class's skills, or finishes a full basic-attack combo and switches to extend it. No elaborate tactical AI required for baseline behavior.
- A completed basic-attack chain has a brief recovery period during which another ordinary basic chain cannot immediately begin. A finisher may knock the target away; exact chain length, recovery duration and displacement vary by kit and remain open.
- Class switching strings into the completed combo and rushes the knocked-back target to continue the incoming class's combo rather than waiting through ordinary basic-chain recovery. The 0.5-second class swap cooldown still applies. Recommend a class-compatible pursuit transition (ranged styles may close to effective range), preserve valid target, and avoid rushing dead/invalid targets. For knockback-resistant bosses, a non-displacing continuation is recommended, not finalized.
- Eligible skills, including supportive skills, emit **Impulse**, a brief hit-stun pulse on activation against enemies within their individual effect area/range. Impulse creates combo continuity and animation-cancel opportunities; it need not deal HP damage. Intact enemy Armor prevents Impulse stagger/knockback even though the hit occurs. Ordinary break/CC rules apply.
- A skill used during a basic string can reset/extend the string, cancel eligible recovery and lead into another basic string or skill. If used following a completed basic string, Impulse knocks back eligible enemies; pursue to continue. This is a combo-state reset, not a skill cooldown reset.
- Skill-to-skill and skill-to-basic transitions may rush/chase displaced targets, close to a skill's effective range, or use appropriate ranged projectile homing. Preserve class/weapon identity and avoid teleporting or rushing invalid targets. Exact movement, tracking and range rules are open.
- Baseline auto-combat intermixes basic strings, eligible skill extensions and swaps through easy-to-author presets and player-created combat macros. Exact sequencing, readiness/Energy gates, cancel windows, pursuit distances, recovery rules and anti-lockdown tuning open; no fixed forced alternation or combo limit established. No automatic cooldown reset or free invulnerability implied.

## Active encounter intervention
Owner requires player-selected combat-zone repositioning against boss/raid attacks in semi-idle combat, short clash QTEs, clickable/tappable exposed weak points for bonuses, environmental hazards and co-op encounter mechanics. No standalone precision-timing QTEs; clash input design remains open. Auto-combat stays available; class evolution trials can be cleared automatically with enough strength. Direct free movement belongs to the later action-RPG alternative. Semi-idle combat retains ordinary automated movement and pursuit; selecting a zone directs the character's repositioning without requiring continuous movement input. Recommend pausing conflicting auto actions during temporary zone-selection/QTE inputs and resuming afterward. Zone geometry, travel time, attack range, target retention, selection UX and auto fallback remain open. Detailed movement override, QTE timing/outcomes, readiness rules and auto fallback are open. Full manual combat remains a separate later scope.

## Automation and macros
Owner requires simple automation, especially easy developer preset authoring, with built-in presets and player-created macros. Recommended implementation: one data-driven, ordered action sequence format for both preset and custom macros, using ordinary combat actions (equipped Core attack or full string, chosen skill, weapon skill, class swap). A shared executor handles target validity, approach/pursuit, weapon compatibility, action recovery, Energy and cooldown readiness. Skip unavailable optional steps or fall back to basic attacks, rather than stall indefinitely; exact skip/wait behavior open. No external scripting or complex tactical AI required. Recommend slot-based references so presets remain reusable across classes, with optional class-specific sequences; advanced conditional commands, sharing, editor limits and unlock rules remain open. Macros use the same authoritative combat rules as normal automatic/manual inputs.

## Compatibility and execution
See [Weapons](WEAPONS.md) for class-linked weapon families. The two equipped weapons remain the same throughout class swaps; no per-class gear replacement. Validate that each distinct selected class can use at least one equipped weapon; no shared family requirement. Automatically select a compatible equipped weapon for actions and class transitions. Skills may be universal across class-compatible weapons, specific to one family, or compatible with multiple families; inherited skills retain requirements. Universal weapon basic animations and per-skill animations are shared across eligible classes. Each weapon supplies one skill, not an additional class kit. Exact basic-attack weapon preference, skill availability validation and off-hand handling open.

- Manual swap triggers, macro editor and exact executor behavior, weapon transition timing and basic-attack weapon preference, cancel/recovery windows, target validity and animations.
- Passive trigger eligibility, talent preset switching restrictions, persistent effects, specialization loadout swap timing, passive-skill trigger scope and duplicate skill eligibility.
- Secondary weapon attribute multiplier, weapon skill activation/slot presentation, duplicate weapon eligibility.
- Slot unlock milestones and out-of-combat loadout editing restrictions.
- Prototype compare same-class specialization vs two distinct classes for uptime, survivability, role utility, automation and readability. Maintain server-authoritative cooldowns and prevent re-equipping/reset abuse.

## Legendary class skill weapon compatibility
Owner direction (2026-11-27): both native Legendary Core Skills and both native Legendary ordinary active skills of a class must work with either of its compatible weapons; no mandatory single weapon for class-defining Legendary skills. Preserve normal skill acquisition and class eligibility. Use weapon-appropriate choreography and skill-defined range, without requiring another equipped weapon. Dedicated weapon skills remain weapon-specific; existing Normal/Rare starting-skill restrictions are unchanged pending further owner direction. Whether rarity promotion of a lower-starting-rarity skill changes its compatibility remains open. Test both weapon loadouts and inherited skill eligibility; do not infer universal cross-class access.
