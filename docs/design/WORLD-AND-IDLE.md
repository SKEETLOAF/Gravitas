# World progression, cooperative play and idle rewards
Updated 2026-11-26. Owner decisions and recommended details distinguished.

## Established owner direction
- World parties up to 4 players. Raids up to 6, with 8 for larger raids. Server bosses server-wide; global event bosses may accommodate any range appropriate to the event (not a claim of unlimited Roblox per-server capacity). Aggro is a character attribute.
- Progression shared by party members in the same location. XP and drops collected by the character along its journey. Exact credit, pickup range, loot ownership, disconnect and eligibility rules open.
- Each map culminates in a strong dungeon to be explored, conquered and looted before entering the next stage. Dungeon goes on cooldown for 10 minutes or Robux skip. Exact trigger, scope, party handling, repeat loot and pricing open.
- Auto-combat available anywhere, optional active input; full manual mode later. Idle systems draw from Go Go Muffin, AFK Arena, AFK Dungeon, Ulala.

## Recommended adventure loop
Map exploration/automatic fights -> collect XP and loot -> build improvement -> conquer map dungeon and boss -> unlock next stage -> improve idle rewards. Can return to earlier maps and replay dungeons. Make dungeon a real traversable set of rooms/encounters with enemy packs, elite fights, optional treasure and boss, not merely a stationary menu fight. Solo and co-op, automation supported throughout. Optional exploration bonuses compatible with automation. Exact room count, duration, checkpoints, progression flags and loot tables TBD.

## Recommended dungeon cooldown
- Per-player per-dungeon 10-minute cooldown starts after successful reward-eligible clear, not on first entry or failed attempt. No cooldown for failing or first-ever clear, and no requirement to wait before advancing to the next map. Owner specifies 10 minutes/Robux skip; details here are proposed.
- Allow helping friends on cooldown without receiving another full repeat reward, so social play is not blocked; alternatively party gate only reward eligibility. Prevent server hopping and rejoin bypass. Make cooldown and next eligibility visible.
- Robux skip affects repeat eligibility, never purchases mandatory first-clear success or bypasses next-map combat requirement. Consider earned skip tokens and a reasonable daily purchase/reward cap; exact caps/prices open. Keep repeat loot economy balanced against free clears. Need owner decision on whether cooldown applies entry or rewards; recommendation is reward lockout for helpers.
- Standard cooldown skips that are deterministic are distinct from direct paid random reward transactions; review Roblox paid-random-items policy if a purchase is effectively buying randomized loot access, and implement required disclosures/eligibility where applicable. No implementation done.

## Recommended idle systems
- Online auto-adventure: live real-time movement and combat, pick up XP/drops along route, progress through eligible regular stages, configurable push/farm/repeat behavior, stop or retry on loss; dungeon gate automation supported but no unlimited repeated dungeon loot.
- Offline expedition/reward chest: calculate elapsed time server-side from demonstrated eligible farming stage/rate; earn XP, currency, materials and appropriate ordinary loot, not automatic first-clear boss progression or duplicate online rewards. Suggest 12-hour initial storage cap for discussion; no fixed cap confirmed. Reconnect claim summary, no loss to inventory overflow, clear estimated rate. Offline farming class credit and equipment drops TBD.
- Quick rewards: optional time-equivalent rewards from demonstrated rate, free daily option and possible earned/paid extra subject to economy and policy. Not required for viable progression. Exact duration, frequency, price, cap open. Avoid double counting real elapsed time.
- Active intervention and build quality can increase demonstrated farming rate; reward advantage follows actual performance rather than a blanket manual multiplier. Players should not need to keep Roblox open overnight. Use offline simulation, not persistent disconnected player bots by default.
- Party progression: same-world/same-location eligible party members get shared clears (including healer/tank contributions, not last-hit only), with individual drops and pickup visuals recommended. Track each player's prerequisite and first-clear flags; catch-up credit behavior and carrying rules unresolved. Recommend no forced loot competition or proximity requirements so strict that support players miss rewards. Offline party credit not assumed.
- Global events: cross-server aggregate contribution if needed; each server obeys platform capacity. Reward participation rather than last hit, scalable boss encounter/health policy, guild coordination, anti-double-claim. Exact player cap, contribution thresholds and timing undecided.
