# World, cooperative play and idle rewards
Updated 2026-11-26. Current conceptual rules; detailed content and tuning open.

## Adventure and dungeons
Each map culminates in a strong dungeon to explore, conquer and loot; first boss/dungeon clear gates next map. Dungeons themselves remain open and farmable; only the boss encounter respawns every ten minutes after defeat. Robux can skip the boss respawn timer, never purchase first-clear success. Repeat dungeon automation farms inside the dungeon between boss encounters. No cooldown on regular dungeon farming. Recommend eligible earned skip tokens, visible timer, no automatic spending, anti-rejoin bypass and sensible party/help credit. Timer scope (personal/party/instance), exact repeat rewards, mob respawn and skip pricing open.
Suggested loop: adventure -> loot/XP -> upgrade -> explore dungeon -> defeat boss -> advance. Real traversable dungeon rooms, enemy groups, elites and optional treasure rather than a menu-only battle; exact topology open.

## Idle and automation
- Auto-combat wherever combat occurs, physically moving and looting. Optional active inputs, full manual later. Owner requires meaningful active intervention in boss fights and raids: player-controlled repositioning to avoid boss attacks, short clash QTEs and optional timing/QTE bonuses. Evolution trials remain auto-clearable with sufficiently strong builds. Recommend temporary manual override followed by smooth auto-resumption; exact controls, telegraphs, outcomes, auto-response behavior and raid difficulty requirements open. See COMBAT-FOUNDATIONS.md.
- Automatic push until blocked, farm last suitable cleared area; player can target farming area or disable advancement. Dungeon repeat farms within dungeon.
- Offline: reduced rewards relative to online, up to 24 hours. Character/class XP, gold and eligible materials; no equipment drops, new dungeon clears or map unlocks. Calculate from demonstrated eligible farm rate; prevent overlapping online/offline accrual. Exact efficiency and material eligibility open.
- Quick Rewards: optional time-equivalent farming rewards, daily free claim, earned consumables and possible paid options. Quantity and frequency open.

## Multiplayer
World parties up to 4; standard raids 6, larger raids 8; server-wide bosses; flexible global events (cross-server aggregate contribution where suitable, not unlimited single-server capacity). Same-location party members share progression. XP/drops picked up by character; individual loot and support participation credit recommended. Prerequisite/first-clear credit, proximity, carrying, matchmaking, raid scaling, disconnect and reward eligibility open. Aggro attribute governs threat, exact formula open. Guilds desired; detailed guild systems open.
