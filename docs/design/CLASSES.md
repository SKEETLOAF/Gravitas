# Class architecture and design template
Updated 2026-11-26. Class expansion established; initial planning scope is two promotions per foundation. Roster names, unlock topology and kits remain open.

## Established direction
- One persistent character per account (no alternate character saves planned at launch) can learn, independently develop and equip multiple distinct disciplines. Promotions unlock new disciplines without replacing prior classes or losing mastery. Sibling branches can remain accessible. Every class must have a distinct benefit, even relative to its ancestors.
- Classes have linked primary/secondary weapons; see [WEAPONS.md](WEAPONS.md). Shared weapons encourage synergy without requiring every compatible class pair to share a weapon (recommended).
- Hybrid classes may unlock through multiple developed disciplines, including advanced branches. Warrior + Cleric -> Paladin is an illustrative owner example, not a roster commitment. Warrior -> Berserker is an illustrative advancement.
- Independent special classes may be obtained via dungeons, events, battle passes or game passes; sources remain candidates. Earnable equivalent power and ordinary progression without payment remain guardrails.
- Character/class progression and promotion requirements are established in [PROGRESSION.md](PROGRESSION.md). Energy and shared combat rules are in [COMBAT-FOUNDATIONS.md](COMBAT-FOUNDATIONS.md).

## Initial promotion roster (proposed)
Two promotions per foundation are established as the initial scope. Owner requires distinct, appealing class fantasies and mechanics that remain connected to their lineage, rather than generic upgrades or repeated roles. All ancestors remain playable. Owner requests Class Change/Prestige stage resets to level 1 for evolution with rising caps (working 60/80/100). Only the displayed evolution-stage level resets; all accumulated growth, talent points, mastery, gear, skills and unlocks are retained (owner-confirmed). Stage levels and prestige are independent per lineage (owner-confirmed); leveling one lineage does not advance another. Catch-up accelerates lagging lineages. Exact unlock topology remains open; see PROGRESSION.md. Independently themed descendants need not imply an obligatory sequence of combat identities. Owner favors Berserker as Warrior's first promotion. Exact progression topology is open.

Each kit should have a compelling primary playstyle and a useful secondary-class contribution through compatible skills/effects/talents, without bypassing trait/passive eligibility. Preserve foundational classes as versatile, consistent options. Firearm availability at launch and afterimage/ward implementation scope require review.

Combined with five foundations and ten ordinary promotions, this gives a 25-class initial design roster, not a release commitment. Core hybrid classes are slated for Prestige III (owner-confirmed direction); exact individual requirements and production/release readiness remain open. ## Inherent attribute growth
All classes receive class-specific natural attribute growth tied to global Character Level, separately from mastery, talents, traits and gear. Owner establishes an 18-point budget across six main attributes, with grades F=1, D=2, C=3, B=4, A=5, S=6. Ordinary focus tops out at A; S requires an additional point trade-off (approved surcharge of one budget point beyond its weight). Canonical growth economy in PROGRESSION.md. Initial foundation profiles below are accepted as a working basis, subject to balancing.

Do not treat these recommendations as restrictions confirmed by owner. Inherent main and soft attribute growth uses primary 100% plus distinct secondary 50%, or same-class specialization at 150% of one primary profile (owner-approved). Apply multipliers to class-derived growth, not shared equipment/companions or a universal baseline counted twice. Exact growth curves remain open.

### Soft attribute proficiencies
Owner accepts the foundational affinities as a working basis with Rogue's Movement Speed replaced by Effect Duration. Proficient soft attributes grow at roughly comparable, modest natural rates across classes; non-proficient soft attributes grow much more slowly. Approved normalized soft-attribute growth targets: 20 units of proficient natural growth or 5 units of non-proficient natural growth, against 75 units of external progression at a comparable benchmark. Proficient and non-proficient are alternative per-attribute rates, not additive contributions to the same stat. These are approximate reference weights, not literal realized percentages of each stat or hard caps. Actual curves, normalization and external investment vary. Equipment and talents are primary build-directed sources; pets and mounts supplement them, cosmetics offer modest bonuses, and future relics may be build-defining. Equipment, pets, mounts, cosmetics, talents and other progression sources supply the bulk of soft-attribute growth. Proficiency is an affinity, not a restriction. Exact rates, units and curves remain open; use the same class-slot multipliers above.

## Common class anatomy
- Class Trait: exactly one inherent passive trait per class, unlocked and upgraded through Class Mastery and shared Ascension items. Not summoned or equipped in a selectable passive slot. Same-class specialization does not duplicate the trait. Class Trait is active only while its associated class is active; explicitly applied effects may finish their intended duration. Exact mastery thresholds, level caps, costs and effects open. Candidate identities (not approved kits): Warrior retaliation, Ranger precision, Mage spell sequencing, Rogue exploiting openings, Cleric divine favor.
- Basic attacks depend on active class and weapon. Shared weapon animation/action templates may have class-specific variations.
- Confirmed per-class loadout: four equipped active skills, one ultimate, one passive selected from two available class passives, plus basic attacks. Once class swapping is unlocked, selecting the same class twice grants both passives without duplicating the active or ultimate kit. Functional base kit and alternate skill acquisition retained. Class Trait is an established separate passive progression track.
- Active skills/ultimates use shared Energy system; individual cooldowns persist across swaps. Ultimate is a skill category, not a separate resource.
- Selectable class passives are distinct from inherent Class Traits and mastery talents. Each class offers two, normally equips one; same-class specialization unlocks both. Both equipped classes' selected passives remain available, but each passive must work with its associated equipped class and respect its class/action eligibility and trigger conditions. Do not automatically apply class-specific skill modifiers to another class; persistent effects may finish normally. Acquisition and upgrade rules open.
- Branching talents and saved talent loadouts are owner-approved. Owner approved five base trees shared across foundational lineages, with Go Go Muffin and Diablo-style inspiration.
- Each weapon has one dedicated weapon skill, requiring its respective weapon to be wielded for execution. Class skills may require a compatible family. Exact activation/wielding transitions and UI open. Basic attacks remain weapon/class dependent.
- Two class selections and two shared weapons; shared gear/companions remain unchanged, class skills independently selected. Duplicate class selection grants scaling specialization instead of a second kit, with possible cooldown reduction. Class swap cooldown 0.5 seconds. Canonical rules in [COMBAT-SWITCHING.md](COMBAT-SWITCHING.md).

## Shared lineage talents (approved direction)
One reusable tree for each of five foundational lineages (Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Rogue, Cleric). All descendants access their lineage's tree, while Class Traits, two selectable passives and individual skill kits provide class-specific identity. Each talent node has 3–5 investable ranks. Each rank adds a linear, explicitly defined effect increment; bonus effective ranks from items, future relics or specialization may exceed the normal investment cap and continue the same progression. Define a safe effect-level cap separately where needed for mechanics such as chances or cooldowns; never silently clamp effective rank to investment cap. No bonus-item or relic design approved by this rule. Individual effects are intentionally modest because invested talents accumulate and trees grow lengthy as more class evolutions are added (owner-approved). Balance total invested builds and cumulative combinations, not isolated nodes. Prefer small incremental attribute, conditional and transformative effects; occasional deeper defining effects are a candidate, not a required capstone system. Exact rank counts and magnitudes open. Recommended one point per rank, with prerequisite ranks or branch-investment gates where useful rather than mandatory maxing of all preceding nodes. Three approved node categories: Attribute (increase existing attributes), Conditional (trigger benefits under combat conditions), and Transformative (modify compatible skill/mechanic behavior). Use reusable mechanical tags (Block, Break, healing, DoT, Energy, etc.) so nodes apply across promotions rather than requiring separate trees. Skill acquisition remains separate from talents through the established base-kit/progression/summon system. Do not add talent-exclusive skills by default. Exceptional unlocks, if ever needed, require separate review. Transformative modifiers may change elements, targeting, area or skill behavior; avoid duplicating ordinary Skill XP/star development. Trees may expand with future promotions, using permanent lineage milestones to unlock later sections; an unlocked descendant must not force a new tree or reset. Promotion-exclusive effects should generally live in its trait/skills/passives, not duplicate talent branches.
- Owner-approved source: earn exactly one talent point per level gained after the talent tree unlocks; no other point-award source. Each lineage earns and owns its own cumulative talent points through its level progression (owner-confirmed). Points do not transfer between lineages. Unlock level and treatment of the unlock-level gain remain open. Earned points are retained across Class Changes (owner-confirmed); recommend reusing capacity per saved allocation, never award a point merely for resetting to level 1; prevent repeat grants for the same earned level milestone. External bonus effective ranks remain distinct from earned talent points. Class Mastery and permanent lineage promotion milestones remain proposed access gates; no separate talent XP grind.
- Saved allocations/presets can be selected for different builds or individual classes within the same tree; reuse unlocked points/nodes without paying again. Respec and preset capacity open; no in-combat talent editing recommended.
- Talent contribution is slot-based rather than active-class-based: primary class allocation contributes full strength; secondary contributes weakened effective ranks, with a minimum of one effective rank for an invested node (interpretation of owner's minimum; verify). Uninvested nodes do not become active by default. Both contributions persist across class swaps, subject to node-specific conditions and compatible actions. Selecting the same class twice empowers the primary nodes instead of granting a second allocation; no duplicate trait or active/ultimate kit. Approved starting balance: secondary talent ranks scale at 50%, retaining fractional effective ranks internally with a minimum of one for each invested node; uninvested nodes stay at zero unless explicitly granted by a bonus. Same-class specialization uses 150% primary talent rank scaling, with no second allocation. Review low-rank breadth incentives during testing. Same-lineage different-class duplicate node resolution and ordering of external bonus ranks remain open; multipliers remain subject to balance testing. Use stable node IDs and linear per-rank effect formulas; preserve investment caps separately from effective ranks. Shared Health/Energy never replenish merely from swaps. The earlier active-allocation-only model is superseded.
- Future hybrid/special classes: recommend access to existing eligible lineage trees rather than creating new trees by default. Hybrid may select one parent tree initially, or mix compatible branches under one bounded budget if desired later; exact eligibility open. No additional independent hybrid talent grind assumed.
- Illustrative branches only: Warrior Fortitude/Martial Mastery/Battle Instinct (broadened after owner questioned Break as a standalone branch; Break-related nodes distributed where relevant); Ranger precision/sustained attacks/mobility; Mage elemental interactions/spell power/Energy efficiency; Rogue criticals/evasion/rapid attacks; Cleric restoration/shielding/enhancement.
- Reference distinction: Go Go Muffin separates skill acquisition (including Class Change summon pools) from level-linked, resettable talents that grant stats and modify current skills. Diablo IV's April 2026 rework emphasizes branching active skill modifications, including changes to element, area and range; passive nodes were removed from its skill tree. Use GGM's separation and consider Diablo's transformative upgrades, not its whole skill acquisition model. Verified references in REFERENCES.md.

## Reusable class design template (proposal)
Use the same template for foundations, advanced, hybrid and special classes. Fill only established details; mark others TBD. Use the confirmed loadout capacities and universal Energy; unique mechanics are class-specific.

1. **Identity:** name, category, parent(s), fantasy, role(s), distinctive playstyle, status.
2. **Acquisition and progression:** prerequisites, unlock path, mastery, branch eligibility, progression retention, alternative paths.
3. **Class Trait:** one inherent upgradeable passive; define mastery and Ascension item requirements, level effects, activation scope, trade-offs and intended solo/co-op value. Do not assume permanent stacking.
4. **Weapons:** primary type, compatible alternatives, shared compatibility, weapon-specific interactions, proficiencies.
5. **Combat foundation:** attack chains, movement, offense, defense, range, resource/gauge if needed, signature loop.
6. **Kit:** inherent passive, active abilities, optional ultimate, weapon techniques versus class techniques, skill modifiers, cooldown ownership. Use confirmed four active / one ultimate / one of two passives; both passives when specializing.
7. **Automation:** easy-to-author default macro/preset, reusable actions and class-specific sequence options, Impulse and swap opportunities, future manual compatibility.
8. **Synergies:** same-class kit, weapon overlaps, non-overlapping functional combos, swap-in/out effects, cross-class resources/buffs/cooldowns.
9. **Growth:** owner-requested inherent class attribute scaling with global Character Level; define a data-driven 18-point letter-graded class growth profile, separately from Class Mastery, traits, gear and talents. Use approved class-slot growth multipliers; exact curves open. Shared lineage talent synergies, mastery milestones, advanced branches, new techniques, horizontal options; later hybrid prerequisites. Do not design a separate tree by default.
10. **Balance and experience:** strengths, weaknesses, solo viability, team contribution, farm/boss performance, comparisons, guardrails. Explicitly identify what makes this class distinct from parents, siblings and hybrids, and what reason remains to use its parent after unlocking it.
11. **Presentation and implementation:** silhouettes, animation, VFX/audio, UI indicators, required assets, dependencies, test cases.
12. **Decision record:** established, exploratory, future, undecided, discarded; next questions.
