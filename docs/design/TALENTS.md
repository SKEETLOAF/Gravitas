# Talent trees

Status: owner accepted the revised conceptual design on 2026-11-27. This is a design specification, not implementation or numerical validation. It supersedes the older shared-lineage, multi-rank, level-10 and 16-TP layouts. Detailed class and universal effects: [TALENT-CATALOG](TALENT-CATALOG.md). Older files under talents/ are historical references only, not implementation input.

## Architecture (approved direction)
- Each individual class (foundation, promotion, hybrid, special) has its own tree. Universal trees are reusable constellation-style networks: a central progression path with short independent one-off branches, without mutually exclusive class-style forks. They are independent of class-specific Traits or named skills and must be competitive with class-tree investment for coherent builds. Both kinds may modify skill properties, provide attributes, conditional effects and major passives. Class trees specialize a discipline; universal trees specialize combat mechanics.
- Initial talent earning starts after P0 level 20. P0 and P I sections cost 30 TP each; P II onward 50 TP each. P0 single-purchase costs range 1–3 TP; minimum node cost increases by 1 per Prestige (P0=1, PI=2, PII=3, etc.). PI class and all universal nodes have a maximum cost of 11 TP, subject to the narrower P0 1–3 range. Later class maxima remain open (owner direction). Later templates below are proposed; no arbitrary prestige tax.
- Each selected tree has an innate entry bonus separate from paid nodes; only selected trees grant it. Three selected trees per preset is a working recommendation, not confirmed cap. Each tree is one continuous network extended by new sections. Class extensions unlock only via its specific lineage's Prestige progression; universal extensions unlock via permanent Character Level at XP-equivalent progression milestones, without requiring any individual lineage to prestige (owner direction). Previously learned trees/purchases retained; descendants have distinct trees, not replacements. Talent points still belong to lineages; global unlocks do not grant extra TP. Exact Character XP curve and prerequisites for a newly acquired promoted tree's prior sections remain open.
- Class sections retain the two independent reconverging mutually restrictive forks; universal sections use central paths with short independent branches. Within each family/tier, uniform paid-node count and cost distribution, not necessarily uniform visual placement or order. Same price sequence/order is acceptable for convenience but not required. Class alternatives must total equal costs and preserve route counts. No mandatory capstone, no monotonic cost ordering, branches can contain minor or major nodes. See uniform templates below.
- Preserve ordinary skill acquisition, separate Class Trait, selectable class passives and collectible passive skills. Talents modify compatible acquired skills, not grant ordinary skills. No new mandatory meters or universal stats from a talent draft. Use canonical Health Regeneration, Penetration etc.; healing/shield/skill effectiveness are effect-specific modifiers, not new character stats.
- Recommended preset/respec UX: outside combat, preserve allocation per preset, preview full prerequisites and incompatible skills, route-aware completion, dynamic modified skill tooltips, future section previews. Exact unlock/respec costs open.

## Point economy (owner-approved 2026-11-27)
Independent lineage TP budgets retained through evolution. Talent earning starts after P0 level 20: levels 21–80 grant 60 TP. One TP per eligible level-up and one per successful Prestige rank-up; no extra award for resetting the displayed level to 1. Later stages earn from entry and levels 2 through cap. No fractional TP, variable rates or bonus milestone grants. Maximum ordinary class stage cap 150; permanent Character Level and continuous XP reference progression are separate.

Full tree means every applicable section through the current prestige. From P I onward each full tree costs exactly one-third of cumulative TP. P0 two trees, later three; zero surplus at the ordinary cap. Three selected trees per preset remains a proposed cap. Equal-cost sections assume native/universal rates; cross-tree premiums and promoted-tree availability require reconciliation.
- Hybrids retain independent progression and tier-equivalent grant. Updated P III entry grant is 331 TP (330 through P II plus one equivalent entry TP); do not award entry twice. Earlier proposed prefill of first two sections of both parent trees costs 120 TP, leaving 211. Prefill itself remains unapproved; immediate access to both parent trees confirmed. Recommend aligning hybrid cap to 150 if same completion target applies, not silently changing it.
- Old 40 native/combined investment gates and 2/3 TP per rank cross-tree prices require reconciliation with single-purchase costs. Ancestral access at native cost is a recommendation. Non-parent access, pricing, respec and preset eligibility open.

## Progression cadence
Owner favors GGM-inspired milestone-led progression and separate uniform templates for class and universal trees. Talent earning starts after P0 level 20. Proposed first/second/third selection milestones at levels 20/40/60, with Class Change quest preparation around 60 and promotion eligibility at 80. Exact access gates remain open. Preserve learned disciplines and no server time locks.

### Continuous tree expansion (owner-approved)
- Class tree: P0 section, then P I continuation, then later continuations unlocked by the specific class lineage's actual Prestige advancement, not another lineage's advancement or account level. For Warrior lineage, reaching P I opens Warrior's P I extension even if another discipline is equipped; whether a newly acquired promoted discipline has all earlier sections immediately accessible is still open. Acquisition of a new discipline is distinct from extension of an existing tree.
- Universal tree: permanent Character Level opens the continuation at the XP-equivalent benchmark of reaching the corresponding lineage stage boundary (P I equivalent: normal cumulative XP to reach P0 level 80; P II equivalent: normal cumulative XP to reach P I level 120 after foundation). No class promotion, trial or mastery prerequisite. Character Level thresholds should be calibrated to the same baseline cumulative XP curve, excluding catch-up modifiers; exact numeric levels depend on the account XP curve. If account and reference curves are identical, illustrative levels 80 and 199 under current continuous reference convention. These are not fixed unlock levels until curves are calibrated.
- A player may unlock universal extensions even while remaining P0 on every lineage. Access is global; spending uses existing lineage TP budget and no extra TP is awarded by account levels or extension unlocks. Already purchased nodes retain their effects; new nodes connect to earlier graph. Any prerequisite investment gates, cross-tree eligibility and promoted-tree initialization remain open.

### Prestige climb and parity
Owner direction: universal paths must be as viable as class paths, especially for players who understand stats and combine them intelligently. Class trees specialize class-specific traits and skills; universal trees specialize cross-class combat systems, stat interactions, conditional mechanics and compatible skill properties. Universal major nodes must not be reduced to bland stat increases or intentionally weaker versions of class mechanics. Well-built universal-only and mixed presets must have credible competing performance within their intended roles; do not require native tree for baseline effectiveness. Whether all-universal presets are selectable is still an access-rule decision; recommend allow.
- Higher-cost nodes should be valuable investments for a coherent or reasonably constructed build, not merely for extreme min-maxing. Evaluate total prerequisite path cost, actual activation rate and marginal build impact, not tooltip percentage alone. Make expensive nodes work meaningfully at ordinary relevant stat investment, with additional synergy at higher investment; cap pathological loops and avoid hard thresholds that make a node dead until unrealistic gear levels.
- Advance mechanical depth with prestige: P0 establish the playstyle/stat foundation; P I introduce conditional interaction and property changes; P II add deeper cross-stat/skill-system interactions and substantial major nodes; later sections expand depth and new options, not automatically inflate percentages. Node prices reflect actual significance; minimum increases one per Prestige, but prices above that floor vary freely, subject to the 11 TP universal/PI class ceiling. No automatic price increase of individual nodes solely based on prestige. Fixed 50 TP later sections mean average prices cannot rise indefinitely at unchanged node count; propose redistributing costs toward major nodes or adjusting density consistently within a family, never shrinking useful choice simply to raise prices.
- Compare equal TP and equivalent equipment: native-heavy, mixed, universal-heavy and all-universal (if allowed); assess clear speed/TTK, resource uptime, effective mitigation/healing, status uptime, boss/farming suitability, auto/manual and group/solo. Evaluate each major node against spending its total prerequisite cost elsewhere; compare role-specific output rather than requiring every build to excel at everything. No numerical parity claimed until combat simulation and playtesting.
- Illustrative major mechanics (proposals, not approved node effects): Warfare uses bounded excess Armor depletion as direct damage or alternate payoff; Tempo changes recovery within animation floors; Flow converts otherwise wasted Energy recovery into a bounded enhancement to a paid action; Endurance leverages effective healing or prevented damage for sustained defense; Opportunity turns effective critical/Accuracy investment into conditional attack properties; Foundation uses base-stat-only bounded defensive scaling. No new generic attributes, uncontrolled stat conversion loops, free cooldown resets or guaranteed status bypass. Existing catalog effects remain proposals and need per-skill/stat resolver validation.

Owner clarification: uniformity means the same node counts and cost multiset per family and corresponding prestige, not identical layout, price order, effect type, or uniformly increasing prices. Equal branch totals remain necessary. Reassess later-stage templates separately; do not force escalating prices or compression under a fixed 50-TP budget. No new P II effects designed yet.

## Multiclass and compatibility
Retain approved primary 100%, secondary 50%, same-class specialization 150% contribution weights. Proposed interpretation for binary ownership: scale eligible numeric coefficients, not the existence of the mechanic; one secondary extra strike at half strength rather than half a strike. Qualitative effects, thresholds, area, count rounding, overlapping universal nodes, external item bonus effects and contribution order require explicit rules. No automatic cross-class Trait or class-specific skill effects; only eligible skills/actions, respecting active class and persistent effect scope. Existing overlap intent retained, no duplicate recursive procs. Use skill tags (projectile, area, persistent, heavy impact, defense, healing, etc.) and per-skill overrides; tags and stacking not finalized. Respect encounter movement zones; auto must be able to use major effects. Modifier order and actual coefficients remain untested.

## Quality bar and references
Owner requires critical evaluation against polished RPGs; do not claim 9/10 without support. Review identity, real specialization choices, compatibility/no dead nodes, cost vs alternative investments including prerequisites, auto/manual utility, readable tooltips/visual effects, stacking/resource loops, implementation complexity, expansion and promotion identity. Conceptual assessment of latest class drafts ~8.6/10 (subjective, not measured). Expensive nodes should provide meaningful new properties or synergies, not merely bigger numbers. Smaller nodes support route identity. Test all four routes against bosses, farming, solo and co-op; balance high-frequency and long-cooldown skills; cap procs, refunds, status spreads, redirection, interception and stat conversion; avoid mandatory routes.
