# Current direction
Updated 2026-11-26. This is a concise overview; linked system documents are canonical.

## Identity and guardrails
- Roblox semi-idle, real-time hack-and-slash RPG with one persistent customizable character per account and learnable/equippable classes, not a hero roster. No alternate character saves planned at launch (owner-approved); appearance/combat presets remain a quality-of-life direction.
- AFK Dungeon-like moving battlefield combat; optional active input (boss/raid repositioning and brief clash/bonus QTEs) and future full manual combat. Hybrid worlds/instances, co-op and guilds.
- Optional depth: casual players including children can use sensible builds and enjoy progression without mastering underlying RPG rules. Advanced optimization is available, not compulsory.
- Paid acceleration is allowed, but free players have realistically attainable equivalent power and guaranteed acquisition paths. No mandatory purchase to progress.
- [References](REFERENCES.md) includes Torchlight: Infinite and Black Desert Mobile as ARPG inspirations, and Sword x Staff for hybrid idle/world exploration, build customization and automated combat.

## Open and deferred
Current priority: class architecture and actual class kits; economy concept established, detailed tuning open. Core foundations and itemization are conceptually covered; do not reopen them without a design need. Numerical formulas, temporary buff/debuff caps, precise promotion/loot/summon rules, detailed swap execution and specific skill kits remain open. Detailed Reforge/Recast (P0) and relic (P II) design, full manual combat and detailed choreography deferred. World setting, final title, individual content, presentation and release scope still to design. See [master tree](MASTER-TREE.md).
