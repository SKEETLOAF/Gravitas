# Weapon roster and class compatibility
Updated 2026-11-26.

## Owner-planned full-game weapon roster
- Sword
- Greatsword
- Polearm
- Dagger
- Mace
- Gauntlet
- Shield
- Bow
- Firearms (Light, Medium, Heavy)
- Magic Staff
- Tome

These are 11 named weapon families, or 13 categories if the three firearm weights are counted individually. No additional families or specific weapon items are confirmed. Not all need to be available at launch.

## Proposed foundational loadout mapping
Five-role promotion architecture is the working direction. Owner requests primary and secondary weapons for each foundation, with Warrior using Sword as primary rather than sword-and-shield as one combined weapon.

| Class | Primary | Secondary | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Sword | Shield | Owner specified |
| Ranger | Bow | Dagger | Bow carried forward from earlier proposed mapping; Dagger owner specified |
| Mage | Magic Staff | Tome | Owner specified |
| Rogue | Dagger | Sword | Dagger carried forward from earlier proposed mapping; Sword owner specified |
| Cleric | Tome | Shield | Tome owner specified; Shield retained as recommendation from prior options |

Early compatibility: Sword connects Warrior/Rogue; Dagger connects Rogue/Ranger; Shield connects Warrior/Cleric; Tome connects Cleric/Mage. All five belong to a connected compatibility network, but not every pair shares a weapon. Shared weapons should encourage synergy rather than dictate class pair eligibility (recommended principle). Mace remains in the full-game roster, currently unassigned to a foundation. Shield is listed independently; whether it functions as an active weapon, off-hand, or both is unresolved.

## Shared loadout (established)
Two global weapon slots shared by the two selected classes. The designated primary weapon contributes full attributes and the designated secondary contributes weakened attributes regardless of currently wielded weapon; approved starting secondary attribute multiplier 50%. This scales stat contribution, not skill coefficients or moveset behavior; subject to balance testing. Each weapon has one dedicated skill requiring its respective weapon to be wielded. The equipped weapons do not change on class swap; selection of active weapon and detailed activation rules remain open. See [Combat switching](COMBAT-SWITCHING.md).

## Open design questions
- Whether secondary means alternative wielded weapon, separate off-hand, or both; compatible weapon selection and secondary attribute multiplier tuning.
- Shared weapon base animations/techniques and class-specific expressions, independent cooldowns and cross-class transitions.
- Whether primary vs secondary differs in proficiency, skill access or progression.
- Firearm weight categories, handling and class compatibility; future weapon assignments, promotion changes, and weapon mastery.
- No specific movesets, stats, attack counts, items, promotion assignments or availability schedule are established.
