# Weapon roster and class compatibility
Updated 2026-11-26.

## Owner-planned full-game weapon roster
- Sword
- Greatsword
- Polearm
- Dagger
- Mace
- Gauntlet
- Shield
- Bow
- Firearms (proposed unified family; skill-driven gun types)
- Magic Staff
- Tome

These are 11 named weapon families. Owner proposes consolidating the former three firearm weight categories into a single equipment family; different guns are determined by skills rather than separate equipped firearm categories. Under this proposal there are 11 total categories. No additional families or specific weapon items are confirmed. Not all need to be available at launch.

## Proposed foundational loadout mapping
Five-role promotion architecture is the working direction. Owner requests primary and secondary weapons for each foundation, with Warrior using Sword as primary rather than sword-and-shield as one combined weapon.

| Class | Primary | Secondary | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Sword | Shield | Owner specified |
| Ranger | Bow | Dagger | Bow carried forward from earlier proposed mapping; Dagger owner specified |
| Mage | Magic Staff | Tome | Owner specified |
| Assassin | Dagger | Sword | Dagger carried forward from earlier proposed mapping; Sword owner specified |
| Cleric | Tome | Shield | Tome owner specified; Shield retained as recommendation from prior options |

Early compatibility: Sword connects Warrior/Assassin; Dagger connects Assassin/Ranger; Shield connects Warrior/Cleric; Tome connects Cleric/Mage. All five belong to a connected compatibility network, but not every pair shares a weapon. Shared weapons should encourage synergy rather than dictate class pair eligibility (recommended principle). Mace remains in the full-game roster, currently unassigned to a foundation. Shield is listed independently; whether it functions as an active weapon, off-hand, or both is unresolved.

## Shared loadout (established)
Two global weapon slots shared by the two selected classes. The designated primary weapon contributes full attributes and the designated secondary contributes weakened attributes regardless of currently wielded weapon; approved starting secondary attribute multiplier 50%. This scales stat contribution, not skill coefficients or moveset behavior; subject to balance testing. Each weapon has one dedicated skill requiring its respective weapon to be wielded. The equipped weapons do not change on class swap. Owner approves automatic action-driven wielding transitions: each equipped class must support at least one equipped weapon; shared weapon compatibility is not required. Basic attack movesets/animations are universal per weapon family, not class-specific. Skills have their own universal animations shared across eligible classes; inherited skills retain requirements and animation. Skill compatibility may be universal across class-compatible weapons, weapon-specific or multi-family. Weapon skills automatically draw their own equipped weapon and retain independent cooldowns across class swaps. Weapon changes alone do not reset combo state, recovery or cooldowns. Exact transitions and shield/off-hand presentation remain open. See [Combat switching](COMBAT-SWITCHING.md).

## Open design questions
- Whether secondary means alternative wielded weapon, separate off-hand, or both; shield/off-hand presentation and secondary attribute multiplier tuning.
- Universal weapon movesets and per-skill animations established; exact movesets, independent cooldown timings and cross-class transitions remain open.
- Whether primary vs secondary differs in proficiency, skill access or progression.
- Gunslinger is a Ranger-lineage advancement with firearm access and skills focused on different guns (owner direction); P II after Hunter (accepted working rule); secondary weapon pending. Recommended unified Firearms equipment family: pistol, shotgun, rifle, automatic and launcher presentations determined by the skill, using shared equipped firearm stats and individual skill animations. Default pistol basic moveset is a proposal; exact basic/weapon skill presentation, compatibility and cosmetics open. Future weapon assignments, promotion changes and weapon mastery remain open.
- No specific movesets, stats, attack counts, items, promotion assignments or availability schedule are established.
